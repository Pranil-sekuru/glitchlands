How it works

  1. Pick a partner at Prof. Semicolon's lab: Byte speaks JavaScript, Pip speaks Python, Luna speaks Lua.
  2. Walk into the tall grass or up to something broken. Every Bug is written in one of the three languages.
  3. Click the buggy line, fix it and press RUN. Your code really runs, in its own language.
  4. Your partner helps with Bugs in their language: all hints and an extra try. Catch a Bug in a new language and its partner joins you.
